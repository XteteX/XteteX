## 🔬 Research

### [Clean Accuracy Explains Most of the Robustness Gap](https://github.com/XteteX/Adversarial-Robustness-CIFAR100)
*M. Oryntay, A. Ghani (Nazarbayev University), 2026, preprint*

A controlled comparison of ResNet-18 and VGG-16 under adversarial training on CIFAR-100 (12 models, 3 seeds, FGSM / PGD / AutoAttack). I decompose robust accuracy into clean accuracy × conditional robustness and show that about 70 % of ResNet-18's robustness advantage comes from its higher clean accuracy, not from being harder to attack.

`PyTorch` `adversarial training` `AutoAttack` · [Paper (PDF)](https://github.com/XteteX/Adversarial-Robustness-CIFAR100/blob/main/paper/Oryntay_Ghani_2026_Clean_Accuracy_Explains_Robustness_Gap.pdf) · [Notebook](https://github.com/XteteX/Adversarial-Robustness-CIFAR100/blob/main/notebooks/adversarial_robustness_cifar100.ipynb)
